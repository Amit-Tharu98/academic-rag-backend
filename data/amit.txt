Q002	What problems can occur when BERT token representations are averaged to create sentence embeddings?	paper1_P008_C000|paper1_P008_C005	"Averaging BERT token representations can give similar importance to semantically important and unimportant elements of a sentence. Q015	What is meant by vector-based semantics?	paper2_P002_C004	"Vector-based semantics represents linguistic meaning using numerical vectors in a multidimensional semantic space. Q016	How are modern transformer-based vector models typically trained?	paper2_P003_C000	"Modern transformer-based vector models learn representations from large collections of natural language text.
